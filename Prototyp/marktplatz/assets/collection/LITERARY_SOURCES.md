# Literary sources · original excerpts

Verified 27 October 2026. Six verse cards and one traditional tongue twister replace the invented verses. Original excerpts retain their wording and punctuation; visual Halloween treatments and learning notes are separate adaptations. Stable IDs are historical keys, not work titles. No modern song lyrics or modern illustrations are copied.

## The Star — Jane Taylor

Card ID: `night-light`. Opening couplet.

> TWINKLE, twinkle, little star,
>
> How I wonder what you are !

[Poetry Foundation: The Star](https://www.poetryfoundation.org/poems/45316/the-star-56d224c697fbe)

The linked transcription credits Ann and Jane Taylor jointly. The Morgan Library attributes this poem to Jane Taylor. [Bibliographic record](https://www.themorgan.org/music-manuscripts-and-printed-music/85839).

## The Moon — Robert Louis Stevenson

Card ID: `moon-spoon`. A Child’s Garden of Verses, opening couplet.

> The moon has a face like the clock in the hall;
>
> She shines on thieves on the garden wall,

## Who Has Seen the Wind? — Christina Rossetti

Card ID: `rain-train`. First stanza.

> Who has seen the wind?
>
> Neither I nor you:
>
> But when the leaves hang trembling,
>
> The wind is passing through.

[Poetry Foundation: Who Has Seen the Wind?](https://www.poetryfoundation.org/poems/43197/who-has-seen-the-wind)
