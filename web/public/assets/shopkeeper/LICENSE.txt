Ollie shopkeeper and portrait — original Lerndorf procedural geometry.
Source: Prototyp/shop/models.mjs:createOllie().
The source project provides this own geometry and its GLB export under CC0 1.0.
The portrait is a local Blender render of that same geometry.
CC0 1.0 Universal: https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/

The stand, lantern, barrel and counter goods use existing Kenney CC0 runtime
assets listed in credits.json; they are not duplicated in this directory.
Kenney: https://kenney.nl/

The recordings in voice/ are separate synthetic ElevenLabs audio and are not
covered by this geometry's CC0 dedication. See voice/README.md and manifest.json.
